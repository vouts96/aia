UNIfied Framework for Ai Impact assessment

The UNIFAI is a comprehensive methodology aimed at evaluating Artificial Intelligence (AI) systems for their impact on society, the economy, and citizens' rights. Through a structured process, AIA examines critical factors such as transparency, fairness, explainability, safety, and accountability of algorithms, ensuring that implemented systems operate ethically and in compliance with regulatory requirements.

This approach is based on risk analysis and prevention of undesirable impacts, focusing on values such as human well-being, equality, and respect for human rights. AIA goes beyond the technical evaluation of algorithms and also considers the social, environmental, and ethical dimensions of their use.

Compliance with Greek and European Legal Framework

This methodology aligns with Greek legislation, including Law 4961/2022, which mandates the preparation of an algorithmic impact assessment for every public sector organization using AI systems. The law clearly states that this assessment must include:

  • The intended purpose and public interest served by the system.
  • The technical characteristics and operational parameters of the system.
  • The type of decisions made with the involvement of the system.
  • The categories of data collected or processed.
  • Potential risks to the rights and freedoms of natural or legal persons.
  • The expected social benefit in relation to potential risks, particularly for vulnerable social groups.

The assessment system that follows is fully aligned with both Greek law and European regulations such as the AI Act, Data Act, and Data Governance Act. Through a dynamic and interactive process, it allows users to thoroughly evaluate their systems, identify potential risks, and ensure compliance with the highest standards of quality and safety.
